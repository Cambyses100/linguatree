Frederick George Philpott

Frederick was born on March 24th, 1922.1   Frederick's father was George William Philpott and his mother was Elizabeth May Fessey.  His maternal grandparents were Richard Charles Fessey and Edith Elizabeth Cox. He had a brother named Roy.  He was the older of the two children.  He died at the age of 84 in July 2006.2 
  • General Notes
      Born 24 March 1922; his June-quarter 1922 Lambeth birth registration names his mother's maiden surname as Fessey (FreeBMD 1d 585). The exact birth date is repeated in his July 2006 Dorset death index. Identified as the son of George W. Philpott and Elizabeth M. Fessey; the parentage is strongly supported by the birth index and their 1918 marriage.
