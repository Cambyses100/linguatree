Henry (Harry) Charles Shafe

Henry, known as Harry, was born about 1893 in Walworth, Camberwell.
  • Birth Notes
      Parish (baptism): Walworth St John the Evangelist Parish: St. Mary Newington, Southwark
Harry's father was William Charles Shafe and his mother was Florence (Florrie) Louisa Webster.  His paternal grandparents were Charles Shafe and Mary; his maternal grandparents were Isaac Webster and Alice. He had a brother and three sisters, named Bill, Florence, Florrie and Maud.  He was the oldest of the five children.  He died on January 6th, 1968 in Folkestone, Kent.
  • General Notes
      Very strongly identified as the son of William Charles Shafe and Florence Louisa Webster. His 1893 Walworth baptism names Charles William and Florence Louisa Shafe; the 1901 census records Harry C. as their son. His 1911 marriage instead names his father as Henry Charles Shafe, box maker, probably a forename error because the occupation matches William and the groom himself was Henry Charles. Served in the Royal Field Artillery during the First World War and was discharged in Glasgow on 10 January 1918 as no longer physically fit. Service description: 5 ft 10 in, blue eyes, brown hair, tattoos on both arms; described as steady, solid and industrious. Occupation on marriage: porter.
