John (James) James Shafe

John, known as James, was born about 1830 in Bury St. Edmunds, Suffolk.1   James' father was John Shafe and his mother was Mary Ann Tuck. He had three brothers and three sisters, named Thomas, Charles, George, Elizabeth, Lewesa and Emma.  He was the second oldest of the seven children.  He died before 1891.
