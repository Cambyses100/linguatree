Charles Fessey

Charles was born about 1820.1   Charles' father was John Fessey and his mother was Elizabeth.  He was an only child.
  • General Notes
      Baptised 28 June 1820 at Priors Hardwick, son of John Fessey and Elizabeth. The 1851 census places him in Priors Hardwick with his parents and brothers; his son Richard Charles was baptised there in 1864 to Charles and Ellen.
