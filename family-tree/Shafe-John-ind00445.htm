John Shafe

John was born about 1804 in Bury St. Edmunds, Suffolk.
  • Birth Notes
      Reported as Suffolk in 1841, Debenham in the unverified 1851 index, Bury St Edmunds in 1861/1871, and St Mary's, Bury St Edmunds in 1881. Search range about 1800-1810; baptism not found.
He died on March 1st, 1890 in Southwark, London.
  • General Notes
      Shoemaker or boot maker. In Bury St Edmunds in 1841 and in Christ Church, Southwark by 1861. In 1881 he and Mary were at the Holland Street almshouses, almost certainly Hopton's Almshouses; he was described as a shoemaker (almsman). His 1 March 1890 entry is in the Christ Church Workhouse death register, with civil death registered in St Saviour aged 87. Parents not ascertained. The London copper-plate manufacturer John Shafe (apprenticed 1794; died 1846) remains a research lead because of the rare surname and because this John's son Charles was a copper-plate polisher. However, the manufacturer's will centres on three minor natural children by Jane Hewitt and supplies no adult son or Suffolk connection, so no pedigree link is justified. Search Shafe, Shave, Shape, Shaff, Shaffe, Sheaf, Schafe and Scafe in Bury, Debenham, Depden and nearby registers, settlement and apprenticeship records. Correction: an earlier tree note wrongly attributed an 1820 larceny and transportation case to John; the defendant was Robert Shafe.
