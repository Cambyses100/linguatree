Esme Dennet

Esme was born on July 12th, 1918 in Kennington, London.  Esme's father was Edmond (Ted) Henry Dennet and her mother was Amy S Kingdon.  Her maternal grandparents were Henry Kingdon and Florence Louise Goddard. She had a brother and a sister, named Eddie and Truda.  She was the youngest of the three children.  She died about 2005.
