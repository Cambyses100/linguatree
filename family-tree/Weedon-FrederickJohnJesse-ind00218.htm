Frederick John Jesse Weedon

Frederick was born about 1889.1   He died in 1951.1 
  • General Notes
      Born about 1889 in Clerkenwell, London. The 1911 census records his full name while he was serving with the 3rd Battalion Royal Fusiliers at Vacoas, Mauritius. Married Lillian Seabourne in Pancras in 1918 and died in the St Albans registration district in 1951. His 1891 household names mother Isabella, whereas the 1901 household names Susannah S.; possible remarriage must be resolved before extending his parents.
