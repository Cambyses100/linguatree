Gertrude (Truda) Dennet

Gertrude, known as Truda, was born in 1914.  Truda's father was Edmond (Ted) Henry Dennet and her mother was Amy S Kingdon.  Her maternal grandparents were Henry Kingdon and Florence Louise Goddard. She had a brother and a sister, named Eddie and Esme.  She was the oldest of the three children.  She died  due to TB at the age of 31 in 1945.
