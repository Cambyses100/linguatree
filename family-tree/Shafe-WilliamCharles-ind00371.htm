William Charles Shafe

William was born about 1868.  William's father was Charles Shafe and his mother was Mary.  His paternal grandparents were John Shafe and Mary Ann Tuck. He had a brother and four sisters, named Henry, Sophia, Mary, Ann and Maud.  He was the second oldest of the six children.  He died about 1930.
  • General Notes
      Effectively proved as a son of Charles and Mary Shafe/Shape: he appears with them at 28 Townley Street in the 1881 and 1891 censuses, and his 1892 marriage names father Charles Shape, box maker. William was a sugar boiler in 1891 and at marriage. Two William Charles Shafe births were registered in 1868, so the correct civil birth entry remains to be resolved. Family recollection: very tall, had brothers, and ran a London business making boxes and packing cases; recollections of his age and death date conflict and need documentary checking.

  • Occupation Details

      From about 1890 to about 1895 William's occupation was Sugar Boiler.
