Mary Ann Tuck (Meriam)

Mary was born about 1805 in Bury St. Edmunds, Suffolk.  She died in 1888 in Southwark, London.
  • General Notes
      John's wife in the 1861-1881 census sequence; named Mary Ann in 1871 and in the 1888 St Saviour death index (aged 83). A strong candidate marriage is John Shave and Mary Ann Turk at Depden on 29 October 1827. Later child baptisms vary the surname as Tuck/Luck, so her maiden surname remains unproved and should be searched as Turk, Tuck and Luck. The 1861 index's 'Meriam' is probably a reading or transcription of her forename.
