Elizabeth May Fessey

Elizabeth was born about March 1892.1   Elizabeth's father was Richard Charles Fessey and her mother was Edith Elizabeth Cox.  Her paternal grandparents were Charles Fessey and Ellen.  She was an only child.  She died in 1948.2 
  • General Notes
      Born at Sheerness and recorded aged nine in Rochester St Margaret in 1901 with parents Richard Fessey and Edith Fessey; her father was a Royal Marine Light Infantry private. Married George W. Philpott in Medway in 1918. Her 1948 London death registration as Elizabeth M. Philpott, aged 55, is a strong match.
