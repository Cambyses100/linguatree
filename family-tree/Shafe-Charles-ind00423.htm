Charles Shafe

Charles was born in 1843 in Bury St. Edmunds, Suffolk.1   Charles' father was John Shafe and his mother was Mary Ann Tuck. He had three brothers and three sisters, named James, Thomas, George, Elizabeth, Lewesa and Emma.  He was the second youngest of the seven children.
  • General Notes
      Recorded in 1861 as John Shafe's son, aged 15, born Bury St Edmunds, and working as a copper-plate polisher. From 1871 to 1891 he is traceable in Newington with wife Mary, later as a box maker; records alternate between Shafe and Shape. This continuity makes the link to John very strong. His specialised 1861 metalworking occupation is a legitimate clue when investigating a possible connection to the earlier London copper-plate manufacturer John Shafe, but it does not by itself establish that relationship.
