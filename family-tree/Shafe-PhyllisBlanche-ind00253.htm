Phyllis Blanche Shafe

Phyllis was born in September 1919.  Phyllis' father was Henry (Harry) Charles Shafe and her mother was Hester Alice Hudson.  Her paternal grandparents were William Charles Shafe and Florence (Florrie) Louisa Webster; her maternal grandparents were Thomas Peter Hudson and Alice C Hornblower. She had three brothers and two sisters, named Harry, Tom, Roland, Hetty and Gladys.  She is the third youngest of the six children.
