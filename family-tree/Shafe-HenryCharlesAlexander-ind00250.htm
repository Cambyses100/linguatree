Henry (Harry) Charles Alexander Shafe

Henry, known as Harry, was born in June 1911 in Kennington, London.  Harry's father was Henry (Harry) Charles Shafe and his mother was Hester Alice Hudson.  His paternal grandparents were William Charles Shafe and Florence (Florrie) Louisa Webster; his maternal grandparents were Thomas Peter Hudson and Alice C Hornblower. He had two brothers and three sisters, named Tom, Roland, Hetty, Gladys and Phyllis.  He was the oldest of the six children.  He died about 1988.
