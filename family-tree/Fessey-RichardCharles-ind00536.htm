Richard Charles Fessey

Richard was born about 1864.1   Richard's father was Charles Fessey and his mother was Ellen.  His paternal grandparents were John Fessey and Elizabeth.  He was an only child.
  • General Notes
      Richard Charles Fessey was baptised 30 January 1864 at Priors Hardwick, Warwickshire, son of Charles Fessey and Ellen. In 1901 he was at Rochester St Margaret, a private in the Royal Marine Light Infantry, with wife Edith and daughter Elizabeth; the family remains together in 1911.
