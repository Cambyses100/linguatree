Cyril (Tom) Thomas Shafe

Cyril, known as Tom, was born on March 15th, 1921.  Tom's father was Henry (Harry) Charles Shafe and his mother was Hester Alice Hudson.  His paternal grandparents were William Charles Shafe and Florence (Florrie) Louisa Webster; his maternal grandparents were Thomas Peter Hudson and Alice C Hornblower. He had two brothers and three sisters, named Harry, Roland, Hetty, Gladys and Phyllis.  He was the second youngest of the six children.  He died at the age of 92 in 2013.
