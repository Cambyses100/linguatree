Reader sections and primary subjects

A page’s subject determines its category. Progress, historical era, complexity and narrative importance do not.

SectionPrincipal subjectMain question
MaterialsNatural, prepared or manufactured physical stockWhat properties and usable stock does this supply?
SkillsPractised physical, perceptual, cognitive or social abilityWhat must a person learn to control or judge?
Tools and EquipmentFunctional objects, components, carriers, clothing and machineryWhat object supplies the function?
Technologies — Processes and SystemsA production workflow or coordinated continuing operationHow do inputs, controls and maintenance deliver the outcome?
Structures and PlacesConstructed site frameworks/infrastructure, plus named locations in a separate atlasWhat is built here, or what does this location permit and constrain?

Reflections is a narrative/strategic section, alongside Survival. About, eras, mission routes and reference notes are other document roles.

Design families rather than use-by-use pages

Use Page Scope and Organisation. One authoritative page covers a design family and its meaningful variants. Construction, operation, inspection, repairs and failure modes belong on that page. A variation is a section unless it has a substantially different mechanism, dependency set or independent use across families.

Atlatl Dart covers blunt practice, fletched and pointed configurations. The recorded green unfletched specimen is one variant, not an invention requiring separate practice-dart and hunting-dart pages. Atlatl includes its simple assembly routes. Plant-Fibre Mats covers a small bedding pad and prospective larger or wearable variants without claiming that all have been made.

Keep Dart Shafts as intermediate stock: straightness, stiffness, defects and moisture concern the material before a dart exists. Keep Atlatl Throwing and Projectile Testing as reusable practitioner abilities. A use label does not create another physical object.

Measurement and other boundary cases

Measuring and recording are skills. Measurement and Records teaches reference comparison, counting, observation and reliable notation. Marked Reference Staff is equipment. A later shared calibration or standards service would be a technology/system when its reproducible references, procedures, responsibilities and checks need a separate page. It is not acquired by making one staff.

Twining is a skill; Plant-Fibre Mats is equipment. Bast Fibre and Bark Cordage are stocks, with Simple Cordage supplying the practised technique. Nettle Retting is a distinct changing batch process with readiness checks, not merely a motor action.

Clay Vessel describes equipment; Clay Vessel Making describes forming and drying; Pottery describes the larger verified ceramic-production chain. Those stages retain separate pages because their controls and acceptance differ. The failed vessel does not demonstrate the whole chain. Pit Firing is a firing operation; Kiln is a constructed firing enclosure.

Structures versus equipment

A structure chiefly arranges a site, creates a usable enclosure or supports a fixed working/resting area: shelter, camp drying framework, kiln enclosure and later built storage, bridges or waterworks. Its page includes location requirements, construction, loads, access, operation, inspection and maintenance.

A tool chiefly performs an object function: knife, vessel, spindle, spear thrower, mat, heating stone, machine or component. Portability alone is not the test. A stationary machine remains equipment; the building and foundations that house it are structures. A fixed camp drying frame belongs with structures; a small removable drying accessory can be equipment.

The three existing constructed-space pages—Basic Lean-to, Drying Rack and Kiln—now live under Structures and Places. Classification does not build a planned kiln.

Places are location records, not capability unlocks

A place records a particular location’s terrain, approaches, water, use, dangers, occupants and dated changes. Projectile Practice Ground is the named trial site in this valley. Selecting and checking a practice site is explained in Projectile Testing, so the local place page need not pretend to be a universal technology.

Traveller’s Camp, Neanderthal Clearing, Meeting Ground and Fallback Bench are atlas entries linked to Valley Map. A visited place does not prove ownership, safety, a supplied refuge or a manufactured asset.

Structures participate in the capability dependency graph. Local places do not: record their use or observation in daily location fields and describe necessary operating conditions in the relevant handbook page. Physical markers already accounted for remain accounted for; relocating a page changes no inventory.

Metadata and browsing

Capability types are material, skill, tool, technology and structure. Place uses type: place and is an atlas role, not a sixth invention category. Each capability has one matching primary tag, valid subtype and one or more domains. Place entries also have domains for cross-topic browsing.

Material subtypes: natural-resource, prepared-material, intermediate-stock, food, fuel. Skill subtypes: technique, judgement, identification, communication. Tool subtypes: implement, container, carrier, clothing, apparatus, component, toolkit, weapon, trap. Technology subtypes: process, system. Structure subtypes: shelter, framework, enclosure, infrastructure. Place subtypes: camp, clearing, working-area, candidate-site.

Folders are 03_Materials, 04_Skills, 05_Tools and Equipment, 07_Technologies and 06_Structures and Places. The last section has distinct Structures and Places subfolders. Function/design-family subfolders organise equipment, including Projectiles. 02_Reflections contains the lunar reviews. Reader order is now 00 About, 01 Survival, 02 Reflections, 03 Materials, 04 Skills, 05 Tools and Equipment, 06 Structures and Places, 07 Technologies, 08 Eras, 09 Reference and 10 Mission. Stable page IDs and aliases preserve identity when sections move.

Stable IDs survive moves and renaming; a historical ID prefix is not the current category. Merged pages retain one canonical ID and old names/path aliases, with removed IDs recorded in the migration manifest. Dependencies link direct requirements for the specified route; no self-dependency is created by an object absorbing its own assembly page.

Topic Index browses the five capability categories together and links relevant atlas places separately. Page Templates, Style Guide and Vault Maintenance define authoring and regeneration. Acquisitions are derived from events, not from folder location or a new name.
