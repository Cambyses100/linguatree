Reference page

Choose the primary subject using Category Specification, then copy the shared metadata and the appropriate category outline. Replace the example values. Required subtype and domains describe the page; unknown dates and dependency lists remain null or empty rather than speculative.

The stable type codes are material, skill, tool, technology and structure; tool displays as Tools and Equipment, technology as Technologies — Processes and Systems, and structure as Structures. Use exactly one primary type tag. Keep the stable ID when reclassifying a page. Subtype values and available topic domains are defined in Category Specification and tools/category_model.py.

Materials

Use type: material and a subtype such as natural-resource, prepared-material, intermediate-stock, food or fuel.

# Example Material
## Stock, composition and condition
## Identification, sourcing and preparation
## Properties and intended uses
## Batch checks and failure signs
## Allocation, storage and losses
## Alternatives and evidence

Describe input versus finished stock, useful quantity, condition and allocation. Link to production methods without treating the finished output as their raw input. Manufacturing does not turn a stock page into an equipment page.

Skills

Use type: skill and subtype: technique, judgement, identification or communication.

# Example Skill
## Ability and scope
## Equipment, materials and prior abilities
## Practice and control
## Feedback, diagnosis and failure signs
## Demonstration, operating limits and further practice
## Alternatives and evidence

Explain what the person must perceive, control and learn. An ordered technique can remain a skill. Give an observable demonstration while keeping practice, mastery and performance in unfamiliar conditions distinct.

Tools and Equipment

Use type: tool and a subtype such as implement, container, carrier, clothing, apparatus, component, toolkit, weapon or trap.

# Example Equipment
## Object, function and scope
## Form, materials and construction
## Assembly or manufacturing routes
## Operation and handling
## Construction and performance tests
## Maintenance, repair and limits
## Alternatives and evidence

Include useful construction instructions on the object page. Link a separate manufacturing process only when it adds a distinct route or substantial workflow. Specify actual capacity and coverage rather than assuming that construction proves performance. Selected natural tools, fixed structures, machines and experimental specimens all fit this category.

Technologies — Processes and Systems

Use type: technology and subtype: process or system.

# Example Process or System
## Outcome or continuing service
## Inputs, equipment, skills and operating conditions
## Stages and controls
## Outputs, completion and acceptance tests
## Time, labour, throughput and ongoing supply
## Failures, maintenance and fallback routes
## Alternatives, scaling and evidence

Explain the organised operation. A batch process has an output and completion criteria; a continuing system also needs replenishment, responsibilities and maintenance. Separate producing a specimen, passing an operating test and establishing reliable supply.

Requirements in prose should explain the YAML links. Alternative methods need their own stated requirements. Dependencies can cross categories in any direction consistent with the actual route. Use only headings that serve the page; do not fill a template with empty boilerplate.

Structures

Use type: structure, subtype: shelter, framework, enclosure or infrastructure. Store under 06_Structures and Places/Structures. Include site requirements, construction/materials, access and loads, operating conditions, inspection, repair and limits. One structure page covers a family and its meaningful variants; constructing it does not demonstrate every operation within it.

Use camp, clearing, working-area or candidate-site subtypes. Describe terrain and bearings, approach/return, water, observed use, occupants/rights, hazards and dated state. Link general methods rather than duplicating them. Omit demonstrated_day and manufacturing prerequisites. A site has no invented inventory.

A demonstration is a wiki-link to the capability actually used. An asset event has asset, change, unit, state and reason. Use stable asset keys such as pot-a or needle-a. Construction, disposal, repair and gifting are separate events. The validator replays quantities. For example: {asset: bark-cord, change: -0.5, unit: m, state: allocated to ties, reason: clothing}. A zero change updates the state of an existing object; it cannot create one.

A food event has lot, change_kg, disposition and reason. Disposition is collected, consumed, processed or lost; calorie accounting uses this explicit field rather than interpreting prose. Weight is edible mass, after shelling or dressing. Separate fresh game by catch so it cannot reappear after consumption. For example: {lot: rabbit-day29, change_kg: 0.3, disposition: collected, reason: dressed catch}, followed by the matching negative cooked-and-eaten event. Add work tasks and their hours to work_budget; work_hours must equal their sum. ongoing_processes holds the latest snapshot of unfinished batches, specimens and unsupplied plans; it grants no assets or outputs. Use Survival Ledger for current stocks and First Month Ledger for the fixed Day 28 starting state. Estimated quantities and patch assumptions belong in the ledger.

Lunar reflection

Use type: reflection in 02_Reflections. Set as_of_day and knowledge_cutoff_day to the same completed lunar milestone day (or Day 1 for the arrival review, half_cycle: 0); moon_phase is full-moon or new-moon, with half_cycle from tools/lunar_calendar.py. Include the common metadata, authored reflective prose and one snapshot block:

<!-- ledger-snapshot:start -->
<!-- ledger-snapshot:end -->

The generator fills ledger_snapshot and the author-quantity table at that cutoff. No asset_events, food_events or demonstrates are introduced. Appraise current capabilities/unfinished work, supply, dangers, bottlenecks, intentions and reasons to reconsider. Future entries are scheduled in the index rather than written with invented outcomes.

Daily moon model fields, including moon_day, are derived by the generator. Survival titles/filenames use Moon N Day M; continuous day remains the inventory and chronology key. The rounded new-moon boundary starts the next labelled moon, as specified in Lunar Calendar. These fields are not nightly observation claims. Link the matching reflection from its Survival day. See Lunar Calendar.

Later narrative

Use project entries with start and end dates or seasons, inputs, workforce, outputs and verification. Do not continue daily reporting by habit once the important dependencies operate on longer timescales. When a Survival illustration is ready, embed it at the beginning of the body and follow Illustration Continuity. The illustration metadata points to the asset from the vault root; the Markdown image path resolves from the note folder. Leave these fields null until an image is selected.
