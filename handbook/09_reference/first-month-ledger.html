Scope and assumptions

This is a generated author continuity model, not measured survival performance. Daily frontmatter is the source of truth. Regenerate with python tools/update_views.py after editing events, then run python tools/validate_vault.py. The original prose is preserved in the pre-revision archive.

The fictional valley has productive hazel patches within a manageable daily circuit in early autumn. Kernel weights are edible mass after shelling; shells are excluded. Gathering and processing take daily labour. Recorded collection is a setting assumption requiring palaeoecological and field review, not a demonstrated regional yield. No imported seed, store or unexplained food subsidy is counted.

The energy column uses explicit rough working assumptions: 6 kcal/g hazelnut kernels, 1.5 kcal/g dressed game or fish, and 2 kcal/g wet acorn meal. These are not extracted food-composition values (see R13 in Research Register). Wet meal composition is especially uncertain. Against an illustrative 2,500–3,500 kcal/day activity requirement, early days can be deficient and later entries are not all assured adequate. Cold, body size, illness, digestibility and work could change the balance. Internal conservation cannot prove long-term health or a realistic gathering rate.

Fuel is dry usable wood mass, excluding wet collected material. Collection and burn estimates are authored, without calorimeter or weather validation. Water columns count completed treatment and untreated drinking separately; evaporation and other water uses are not modelled. Initial untreated drinking is a survival risk, not a recommended procedure. Zero untreated intake means treatment was recorded, not independently certified.

Daily food continuity

Kernel use includes an explicitly recorded damp loss on Day 13. All retained fresh game is cooked and consumed on its catch day, including the limited 0.90 kg fox lot on Day 23. Discarded trimming is excluded from edible inputs. Acorns enter as 0.40 kg unprocessed input on Day 17, convert to 0.65 kg wet meal on Day 18 (water adds mass), and are eaten over Days 18–19. The input lot contributes no calories before processing.

Important events and limits

  • Day 3: first ignition; the fire kit needs continued maintenance. Stored heat is not permanent access to fire.
  • Days 4–5: first vessel heat trial fails, then repair permits recorded boiling. Day 9 wear interrupts treatment; Day 10 repairs restore it.
  • Day 7: the awl works. A needle attempt fails; a usable eyed needle follows on Day 12.
  • Days 9–10: the first rabbit pelt becomes a small carrying sling; the load test is under one kilogram.
  • Days 11–16: a small pot is shaped, dried, fired, cooled and rejected for leakage. It remains a non-food sample; no ceramic cooking asset is counted.
  • Days 4–20: the nettle batch is retted, processed in two halves, and partly made into cord. The first processing date is Day 16; elapsed time is twelve days, not a universal recipe.
  • Days 13–14 and 24: small hides become a pelvic pad and narrow shoulder panel. They do not amount to a winter coat.
  • Day 21: a wooden spindle bypasses ceramic success. A few metres of single yarn do not demonstrate a sustained productivity improvement.
  • Day 23: the damaged snare is removed. A limited fox meat lot is cooked and eaten, with additional work and fuel; its damaged pelt becomes a limited panel on Day 24.
  • Day 25: the first approach is rebuffed. Samples and the first spindle are abandoned during retreat, not accepted gifts. Day 26 records self-repair and planning, with no fibre exchange or shared teaching. No communal provisions or adoption are established.
  • Days 27–28: a replacement spindle is made. Two green dart candidates, a feather lot and an untested wooden atlatl remain distinct assets.

The earliest practical review should test food yield, bark-vessel reliability, heating-stone selection, fibre throughput and rawhide performance. Research Register records the evidence gaps; Civilisation Roadmap keeps later development tied to surplus and verified capabilities.
