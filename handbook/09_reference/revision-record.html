Main editorial choices

  • Retain approximately 40,000 BP and northern Iberia; label the exact valley, weather and encounters as authored choices.
  • Clarify transit as living-body-only, and provide prior practical training without granting mastery.
  • Make hazelnuts the explicitly accounted first-month staple; game remains supplemental.
  • Replace unsupported water pasteurisation, raw-organ meals and preserved-meat stock with observable boiling and same-day game consumption.
  • Retain a ceramic pot trial but remove it from the indispensable route. A wooden whorl provides the first spindle demonstration.
  • Separate successful construction from operational performance and community adoption.
  • Replace deterministic status displays with cautious, reciprocal first contact.
  • Track state through daily events and verify conservation automatically.
  • Expand the mission roadmap through measurement, surplus, institutions, industry and space systems.

Remaining limits

Reference notes have been edited for factual claims, dependency logic and practical scope. They remain drafts where detailed experimental verification or identification evidence is absent. The resource ledger is an internally consistent author model, not a demonstrated survival plan. Open setting decisions remain in Setting Specification.

The archive contains the removed prose and historical claims. Existing daily filenames are retained so external links do not silently break; displayed titles may describe the revised emphasis.

The first month ends with an assembled but untested wooden thrower, green shaft candidates, a replacement spindle, a modest nut reserve and tentative reciprocal contact. Successful pottery, dependable fishing, large-game hunting and community-wide adoption are not claimed.

These are editorial, structural and model-continuity checks. Dataview rendering has not been tested inside Obsidian, and the improvised techniques and assumed local resource yields have not been experimentally validated. No website was built or published in this revision.

Next substantive work

Develop reciprocal learning and seasonal preparation before asserting a stable specialist economy. Priorities are protection for feet and exposed skin, dependable shelter and water equipment, food yield and storage evidence, shared communication, and repeated independent production. Projectile trials are one bounded experiment within that work.

Later chapters should change scale to weeks, seasons and projects as appropriate. The expanded roadmap restores the original technical mission, with explicit route comparisons and acceptance tests through industrial production and spaceflight.
